THE ROLE OF SMPS

The Smart Money Positioning System (SMPS) serves as the capital management engine of the TBB Framework.

Every deployment cycle passes through SMPS before execution begins.

The system continuously monitors capital deployment, cumulative investment, projected return, expected profitability and operational boundaries.

Its objective is not prediction.

Its objective is structured capital allocation throughout the entire expedition.

CORE FUNCTIONS

CAPITAL MANAGEMENT

Monitor deployed capital throughout every execution cycle.

EXPOSURE CONTROL

Track operational exposure and remaining capacity.

PROFIT PROJECTION

Calculate projected gross return and expected profit.

CYCLE MANAGEMENT

Maintain progression continuity and reset discipline.
