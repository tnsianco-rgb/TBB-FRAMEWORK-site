What Is The TBB Ecosystem?

The TBB Ecosystem is the operational environment surrounding the TBB Framework.

It was developed to organize observation, progression tracking, positioning architecture and execution into a single structured environment.

Rather than focusing exclusively on outcomes, the ecosystem focuses on understanding the structures that produce them.
